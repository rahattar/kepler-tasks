# Pump bank P1-P10: installation sheet (instrument tappings)

All ten pumps draw from the intake basin through their own suction lines and discharge through a check valve and
an isolating valve into the common discharge header. Each pump has a suction pressure gauge tapping and a discharge
pressure tapping; the discharge tapping is upstream of the check valve.

| item | value |
|---|---|
| suction pipe at the gauge tapping, inside diameter | 350 mm |
| discharge pipe at the gauge tapping, inside diameter | 250 mm |
| discharge lines, pump to header | DN250 steel, 273.0 mm OD x 9.27 mm wall, cement-mortar lined |
| discharge tapping above suction tapping, P1, P3-P10 | 0.80 m |
| discharge tapping above suction tapping, P2 (relocated 2024) | 2.10 m |

Transmitters are mounted at the tapping elevation; readings are gauge pressure in kPa.
