# CT-2 water circuit

Hot water from the process returns along a single supply header. The header flowmeter sits upstream of every branch. Downstream of it, each of the eight cells is fed by its own riser, which has a flowmeter and, above the riser's tee off the header, a hot-water thermocouple. The riser feeds the cell's distribution basin and nozzles, and the water falls through the fill into the cell's cold-water basin. It leaves the basin over the outlet weir, where the cell's cold-water thermocouple is mounted. The weirs of all eight cells discharge into a common outlet flume, which runs to the circulating-water pump pit.

Cell C1, the windward end cell, also has a 6-inch freeze-protection bypass. It tees off the supply header downstream of the header flowmeter and discharges straight into C1's cold-water basin, ahead of the outlet weir, without passing the fill. It is opened for cold start-ups and is otherwise kept shut.
