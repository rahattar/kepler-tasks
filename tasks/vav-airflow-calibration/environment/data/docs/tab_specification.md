# Building C, level 3: air balance requirements (owner's standard, section 23 05 93)

- Every VAV box is flow-calibrated at its design minimum and design maximum airflow.
- Acceptance: with the calibration loaded, the airflow delivered at the box's diffusers is within 10 percent of the
  setpoint at design minimum and at design maximum.
- Airflow is measured with a capture hood at every diffuser the box serves; the box value is the sum.
