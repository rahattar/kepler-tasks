# Noise permit, excerpt (conditions 6 and 7)

6. Turbines T04 and T05 operate in sound mode S2 from 22:00 to 06:00 local standard time every night: active power is limited to 1800 kW.

7. The operator keeps the sound-mode schedule in the turbine controllers. Sound-mode operation is not a fault and is not reported as a separate status.
