# Borefield extension, 20-year fluid temperatures: pre-mortem, promising (Nov 14)

A moving finite line source with the surface mirror, averaged over the receiver; the double integral is
reduced to one over depth difference, with u = r sinh s (Gauss-Legendre on the 1/R peak fails without it).
FLS matches ILS to 0.3 per cent at 10 h to 30 d. `mfls.py`, `pilot.py`, `pm2.py`.

| site | misfit of the existing 4x4 field's 5-year record (noise 0.05 K) | 20-year minimum of the 8x8 target, error by route |
|---|---|---|
| groundwater 1e-7 m/s | stated model 0.38 K; lambda refit 0.31 K; vD fit 0.06 K | stated 5.8 K; lambda refit 5.0 K; vD fit 0.2 K |
| heat meter set for water on glycol (x1.07) | stated 0.43 K; lambda refit 0.07 K; vD fit 0.35 K | lambda refit 1.4 K; vD fit 4.1 K; scale fit 0 K |
| both | stated 0.72 K; (lambda, vD) 0.06 K; (scale, vD) 0.06 K | (lambda, vD) 1.0 K; (scale, vD) 0.3 K |

- The 72-hour test does not see groundwater (0.149 K per W/m at every flow), and the first year moves by 2.4 per
  cent. At 20 years an 8x8 field moves by 46 per cent. The deliverable is far more sensitive than the evidence.
- A meter scale and a higher conductivity fit the field record alike. Only the stated rule (the site's
  conductivity is the test's) and the test's accuracy separate them.
- Risks: a reviewer may call a conductivity refit sound practice (put the test in one of the field's own
  boreholes); the band against the reference's precision needs hourly records; a low run may fit scale and
  groundwater directly.
