# Room check of the survey: reference sound source RSS-1

A reference sound source was stood on the floor in the open part of the hall, clear of every wall by more than
1.5 m, and the level around it was measured on a hemisphere of 3.0 m at the six positions R1 to R6 of
`rss_levels.csv`, with instrument M-2, in the same pass as the rest of the survey. The levels with every machine
and the source off are those of `background_levels.csv` away from the louvred wall.

Calibration certificate of RSS-1, sound power level by octave band, as supplied:

| Band (Hz) | 63 | 125 | 250 | 500 | 1k | 2k | 4k | 8k |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lw (dB) | 91.2 | 93.6 | 95.1 | 96.0 | 96.3 | 95.4 | 93.7 | 90.2 |

The source was run alone, with all eight packages shut down.
