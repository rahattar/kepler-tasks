Against v6 (low 1 of 12, xhigh 1 of 6):

- **xhigh rose from 1 of 6 to 3 of 6.** All three passes made the intended argument before ordering: the two
  foot-mounted jacketed models ran at 0.38 and 0.47 of the pumped temperature in 2019, so each gets a visit;
  the two centreline models both ran at 0.16, so one visit covers both. Six runs a side does not separate
  3 of 6 from 1 of 6 on the count alone. What does is where the sixth visit went: a compressor in five of six
  xhigh orders on v6 and in one of six here.
- **Low did not follow, as far as six runs can show.** 1 of 6 here and 1 of 12 on v6 are the same rate within
  the noise; pooled, 2 of 18. Four of six low-effort runs still named K-201A.
- **Without the reassurance the spare goes to more places.** Compressor 5 of 12, a second lagged pump 3 (probes
  3, 7, 11), the pump whose grout was repaired 2 (probes 2, 10). A run that doubts the lagging was done alike
  checks the lagged class across models and has nothing left for the jacketed one.
- **A new miss, ten trains wide.** Probes 2 and 11 visited no jacketed pump at all. Probe 11: "with the jackets
  drained I treated them as plain pedestals". Nothing in the records says that; the drained pedestals run at
  0.80, 0.66 and 0.43 against 0.62 and 0.30 for plain ones. Residuals 0.11 to 0.52 mm on ten trains.
- **Every failing run that covered the classes missed the same two trains,** P-320A and P-320B, by 0.36 to
  0.40 mm against 0.05. Probe 7 said so in its own report: "a P-320 visit would have been worth more than
  P-310B ... The order can't be changed."
