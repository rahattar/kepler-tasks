Clean-context probe runs on the sixteen-station fleet. Each agent got only the brief,
the inspection data and the bench client in an isolated directory -- no sealed physics,
no grader, no reference -- and its recipe was graded afterwards with the shipped
verifier. Every run spent the real budget of eight bench runs.

WHY THESE PROBES LOOK THE WAY THEY DO. The previous fleet (twelve stations, three lots,
a 20% allowance) was solved by the platform's own easiness screen three times out of
three, at 17 to 43 minutes and 13k to 32k output tokens per attempt. An earlier set of
local "low-effort" probes had been capped at 30 tool calls and one shot at the recipe,
and had predicted a pass rate of one in six; they were crippled far below what the
screen actually allows, and calibrated the task against the wrong thing. The probes
below are matched to the screen instead: about 60 tool calls and 30 minutes, free to
check and revise. Full-effort probes get about 200 tool calls and two hours.

The probes were graded when the verifier resolved the wafer in 40 rings. It now uses
80 (method_study.txt). The five round-2 recipes were re-graded on the 80-ring
verifier and every verdict is unchanged: A1 16/16, A2 15/16 (lost S12), A3, B1 and
B2 14/16 (lost S09 and S12), with dwell ratios no higher than 1.073. The figures below
are from the original grading.

  Screen 4 of 6, full 2 of 2. Below the bar, and the reason is measurable
  (allowance_window.txt): at a 20% allowance a recipe centred in its pressure band
  tolerates a coefficient estimate 13-17% low or 14-20% high, because the optimum dwell
  does not depend on the coefficient at all and the coefficient only places the
  pressure. Any covariate model that gets the shape roughly right lands inside that. The only thing
  failing agents was a gross forward-model error -- the twelve-lot hinge structure was
  invisible behind the tolerance.

  Every one of the six agents that built a sound forward model found the
  pressure-accommodation degeneracy unprompted, and every one of them found the hinge.

  Every failure is the same two stations, S09 and S12, on the zone limit by 0.65-0.98
  K: their accommodation coefficient predicted low. Both sit on the saturated plateau
  at low roughness. Every campaign above also ran S05 first or second -- the lowest
  roughness plate, and half of the only same-lot pair with a large roughness contrast,
  so the natural anchor for the roughness slope -- and in the shipped draw S05 is a
  -2.9 sigma outlier (design_mc.txt). A slope anchored on it bends every low-roughness
  prediction on the plateau. The seed has been 20260919 since the first version of this
  task; the outlier was always there and only became visible once the throughput limit
  was tight enough for a few percent to matter.

  The two passes are the two campaigns that reached that corner directly. A1 got there
  by optimal design: it held back its last two runs and spent them where its candidate
  models disagreed most. B1, a full-effort run, identified exactly the right risk and
  could no longer act on it. That is the engineering judgement the task is built to
  test -- spend the irreversible budget where your prediction is weakest, not where
  the structure is most obvious -- and it is not reliably bought by effort alone.

WHAT THE TWO ROUNDS TOGETHER SAY

  * The forward model, the pressure-accommodation degeneracy and the hinge are all
    found reliably by screen-effort agents. None of them is a source of difficulty
    on its own.
  * What separates pass from fail at 8% is where the campaign spent its runs, and to a
    lesser degree whether the agent regressed the physical accommodation coefficient
    rather than a lumped conductance (design_mc.txt quantifies the second as the
    smaller effect, and one that only matters for a campaign that missed the corner).
  * S09 is one of the seven vertices of the fleet's convex hull in (roughness, bath
    temperature). No failing campaign measured it, or its lot-mate, so each
    extrapolated to the corner its prediction was weakest at. This is what the
    reference now plans its campaign from: every hull vertex measured, so every
    unmeasured station is an interpolation (design_mc.txt).
  * Measured pass rates at 8%: 2 of 3 screen, 0 of 2 full. Five runs is too few to
    claim an effort gradient in either direction, and none is claimed. What it does
    show is a pass rate well away from both 0 and 1, decided by a recognisable
    experimental-design choice.
