# PS-4 Flat wall panels

Plant standard for the flat walls of tanks, casings and ducts that carry a pressure difference.

## 1 Scope

PS-4 applies to a flat rectangular wall panel of uniform thickness that is welded along all four edges to the
members that bound it (frame sections, stiffeners or the adjoining walls), where

- the longer side is between 1 and 3 times the shorter side;
- the shorter side is between 25 and 500 times the thickness;
- the wall is of a metal in the unit's materials table;
- the wall stays elastic at every condition on record.

## 2 The panel

2.1 A panel is the part of the wall between the centre lines of the members that bound it. Its sides a and b
(a the longer) are measured between those centre lines, and t is its thickness.

2.2 The bounding members are stiff against bending, twisting and stretching in comparison with the wall.
Along each of its four edges the panel does not deflect, does not rotate about the edge and does not move in
the plane of the wall.

2.3 The wall is a thin plate: the stress through its thickness is neglected, and a line normal to its middle
surface stays straight and normal to that surface.

2.4 The material is isotropic and linearly elastic, with the modulus of elasticity and Poisson's ratio of the
unit's materials table.

2.5 Without pressure the panel is flat and free of stress.

## 3 Load

A condition puts a pressure difference across the wall. It is uniform over the panel and acts normal to the
wall.

## 4 Results

For each condition of each panel:

- **deflection**: the displacement of the centre of the panel normal to the wall, in mm;
- **edge stress**: the stress at the middle of a long edge, acting across that edge (in the direction of the
  shorter side), at the face of the wall where it is tensile, in MPa.

Results are calculated to within 0.2 per cent.
