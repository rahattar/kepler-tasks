# Boiler B2 secondary superheater: arrangement

The secondary superheater outlet bank hangs in the horizontal pass above the furnace exit. Its ten elements, E1 to E10, are numbered from the left side wall to the right. E1 to E6 belong to the primary pass, fed from inlet header A; E7 to E10 form the final pass, fed from inlet header B. Each element's outlet leg rises through the roof into the penthouse, where a pad thermocouple is welded to it.

Elements, grades and design flux factors are in element_register.csv. The design flux factors are the boiler maker's map of the relative heat flux at the outlet-leg crowns at the original firing arrangement.

Soot blowers in the outlet bank:

- IK-22, in the cavity beside E10 at the right side wall, in service since commissioning.
- IK-14, in the cavity beside E1 at the left side wall, added in 2021.

The crowns are reached for UT from scaffolds in the cavities and between the elements.
