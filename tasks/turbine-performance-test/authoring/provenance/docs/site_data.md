# Site data

GT-1 is a single-shaft heavy-duty gas turbine generator, simple cycle, natural gas fired, with an inlet filter house
(pre-filters and final cartridges) and a silencer. The plant datum, at the compressor inlet centreline, is 310 m above
mean sea level. The met mast stands 40 m north of the filter house; its sensors are at 2 m above grade (grade is
plant datum minus 4 m).
