The allowance is 2 readings. Four facts want one:

  gc 2       no record settles it
  gc 3       on record: the third stage's separator is bypassed, so its gas is the second stage's
  weigh 2B   on record: the overhaul register's shop ticket gives the new piston against the one removed
  weigh 3B   no record settles it

So the two readings go on gc 2, weigh 3B, and the other two come out of the records.

| plan | readings | right of 12 | what it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| the analysis of the second stage and the weighing of 3B | gc 2, weigh 3B | 12 | nothing |
| the same two, booked in the other order | weigh 3B, gc 2 | 12 | nothing |
| the analysis of the third stage and the weighing of 3B | gc 3, weigh 3B | 12 | nothing |
| both analyses, no weighing | gc 2, gc 3 | 10 | 3B pressure, 3B limit |
| both weighings, no analysis | weigh 2B, weigh 3B | 10 | 2A pressure, 3A pressure |
| the analysis of the first stage, which the records already hold, and the weighing | gc 1, weigh 3B | 10 | 2A pressure, 3A pressure |
| the weighing of 2B, whose difference the register gives, and the analysis | gc 2, weigh 2B | 10 | 3B pressure, 3B limit |
| one reading: the analysis only | gc 2 | 10 | 3B pressure, 3B limit |
| one reading: the weighing only | weigh 3B | 10 | 2A pressure, 3A pressure |
| no readings at all | none | 8 | 2A pressure, 3A pressure, 3B pressure, 3B limit |

A plan that spends a reading on a fact the records already settle leaves one that they do not, and the
allowance cannot be refilled: environment/rig/lab.py refuses the third reading (see reference_run.txt).
