# Section 23 31 00 HVAC ducts and casings (excerpt, 2025 renovation issue)

3.4 Flexible connectors at terminal units

- Maximum length 5 ft, installed fully extended.
- Support at intervals not exceeding 4 ft with saddles at least 1.5 in. wide; sag between supports not more than
  0.5 in. per foot of length.
- No bend sharper than one connector diameter in radius within 2 ft of the terminal inlet.
