Before work starts on the sixteen zinc-plated flanged joints, J01 to J16, the crew needs an assembly torque for each one. Specify a value in N.m for every joint, to be applied to every bolt in its full pattern.

The read-only records are under `/app/data`. Joint details and the target preload per bolt are in `/app/data/joint_schedule.csv`; the bolt-up procedure is in `/app/data/docs/procedure.md`. The fastener data are in `/app/data/fastener_handbook.csv`, and notes for each joint are in `/app/data/maintenance_log.csv`. Two earlier assemblies of this flange type are recorded in `/app/data/examples/E01_record.csv` and `/app/data/examples/E02_record.csv`, with trial torque and preload, the assembly torque used, and per-bolt preload after the full pattern was tightened.

At the rig, `trial <joint_id>` tightens one bolt to the handbook torque for that joint's target preload. To choose the torque instead, the command is `trial <joint_id> <torque_Nm>`. Adding the word `pattern` (`trial <joint_id> pattern` or `trial <joint_id> <torque_Nm> pattern`) tightens every bolt of the joint's pattern to the torque and reports the preload then measured in one monitored bolt. All commands run from `/app` and print the torque applied in N.m and the preload in kN. Only J01 to J16 are available. The allowance for the whole job is 8 trials: a single-bolt trial uses one of them and a pattern trial uses two. The rig refuses further trials once that allowance is used.

Put the assembly torques in `/app/output/torques.json`, using the structure `{"torques": {"J01": <N.m>, ..., "J16": <N.m>}}` with a numeric value for every joint, each appearing once. At least 15 of the 16 joints must meet both assembly requirements: after the full pattern is tightened, each bolt's preload must be within 6 percent of the schedule's `target_preload_per_bolt_kN`, and no bolt in that joint may have been taken beyond its proof load.
