The one choice a hedge survives is "residual stress from the certificate yield, or from the grade's specified minimum": on all 5 cracks it affects the two lives differ by only 1.04 to 1.11, inside the window's ratio of 1.25, so a single interval fits both without splitting any crack; the choice is a sensitivity the window absorbs, and the hedge still needs every other part of the model right.
