# Raw Mill Fan - OEM datasheet (as built)

- Single inlet centrifugal fan, overhung impeller, direct drive from a 6-pole motor through a flexible disc coupling; variable speed drive, maximum continuous speed 1180 rpm.
- Shaft drawing FS-4122 rev A; sections from the coupling end of the shaft:

| from (mm) | to (mm) | diameter (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 300 | 100 |
| 300 | 500 | 140 |
| 500 | 1400 | 160 |
| 1400 | 1600 | 140 |
| 1600 | 1950 | 130 |

- Bearings: spherical roller bearings, drive end at 400 mm and fan end at 1500 mm (bearing centerlines); radial stiffness 0.80 GN/m each.
- Pedestals: two cast pedestals of one design, effective mass 160 kg each at the bearing centerline.
- Coupling hub (fan half): center of gravity at 60 mm; mass 24.0 kg; transverse moment of inertia 0.35 kg m2; polar moment of inertia 0.60 kg m2.
- Impeller: center of gravity at the shaft end, 1950 mm; mass 550 kg; transverse moment of inertia about the center of gravity 53.4 kg m2; polar moment of inertia (WR2) 97.0 kg m2.
