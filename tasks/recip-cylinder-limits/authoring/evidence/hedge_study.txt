Hedging, choice by choice. A value can be hedged when one entry is inside the band under both answers:
for a pressure, when the two answers are within two tolerances (1.0 per cent) of each other; for a
requirement, when both answers name the same one.

Choices a whole sheet can hedge: pin reversal: checked or not.
Nothing in the answer turns on them, which is why they can be.
Every other choice moves a value past the band under one answer or the other, so no single sheet is
right whichever way it goes.
