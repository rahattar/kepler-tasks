# Dryer Exhaust Fan - OEM datasheet (as built)

- Single inlet centrifugal fan, overhung impeller, direct drive from a 4-pole motor through a flexible disc coupling; variable speed drive, maximum continuous speed 1480 rpm.
- Shaft drawing FS-4123 rev A; sections from the coupling end of the shaft:

| from (mm) | to (mm) | diameter (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 300 | 100 |
| 300 | 500 | 135 |
| 500 | 1550 | 155 |
| 1550 | 1750 | 135 |
| 1750 | 2270 | 125 |

- Bearings: spherical roller bearings, drive end at 400 mm and fan end at 1650 mm (bearing centerlines); radial stiffness 0.95 GN/m each.
- Pedestals: two cast pedestals of one design, effective mass 220 kg each at the bearing centerline.
- Coupling hub (fan half): center of gravity at 60 mm; mass 24.0 kg; transverse moment of inertia 0.35 kg m2; polar moment of inertia 0.60 kg m2.
- Impeller: center of gravity at the shaft end, 2270 mm; mass 400 kg; transverse moment of inertia about the center of gravity 44.6 kg m2; polar moment of inertia (WR2) 81.0 kg m2.
