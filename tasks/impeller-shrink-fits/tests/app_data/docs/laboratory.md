# Materials laboratory

    cte <material> <T>

reports the mean coefficient of expansion of a sample of the material from 20 C to T (100 to 450 C), from a
dilatometer, with a repeatability of 0.3 % (one standard deviation). Materials: FV520B, Ti-6Al-4V, 42CrMo4,
26NiCrMoV14-5 (the rotor forging).

Run it from `/app`; each report is printed and saved under `/app/lab_reports`. The laboratory is booked for 3 tests
in all.
