# cooler-fouling-assessment

Ten shell-and-tube glycol coolers (E1-E10, glycol on the shell side, cooling water in the tubes) need an as-found
fouling resistance each on 2026-10-26, within 0.04 m2K/kW, before the outage cleaning plan is made. The records in
`/app/data` are the unit register, the maintenance log, the March 2025 post-cleaning acceptance tests, four weeks of
hourly DCS trends per unit (flows, water inlet and glycol inlet and outlet temperatures, tube-side dp, run or
standby) and seven documents (thermal design sheet, the new unit's vendor datasheet, the ribbed-tube retube summary,
the E5 replacement-bundle note, instrument notes, the clean-and-test procedure and the requirement). The maintenance contractor can clean and test three units on the day: an as-found
test with calibrated instruments and the DCS readings, then hydroblasting and a repeat test. The deliverable is
`/app/output/fouling.csv` (unit, fouling_m2k_per_kw).

## Difficulty

In practice this is the work of a plant thermal or reliability engineer who has to put a fouling figure on every cooler of a bank before an outage, with only a few units that can be opened and tested. The bank, its records, trends and test readings are synthetic, written by the disclosed generator in authoring/provenance from the sealed definition.

The records settle most of the bank. Each unit's 2025 acceptance test, made just after hydroblasting, gives its clean performance, and the design sheet's film-coefficient exponents carry it to the flows of every hour in the trend; the fouling resistance is 1/U from the trend minus that clean 1/U. Several things in the records must be read correctly. The glycol-outlet RTDs are off by up to 2 K, differently per unit; standby hours (water shut, glycol circulating) show each unit's offset. The cooling water of all units returns to one header with no per-unit outlet temperature, so the duty has to come from the glycol side. One unit's fouling grows fast after a chlorination outage and steps down at the shock chlorination of 5 October (biofilm stripped, growth carrying on after it), so its trend has to be carried two weeks forward with a line and that step (the four-week median misses by 0.09 m2K/kW, the last two days by 0.13, one straight line through the four weeks by 0.07, through the last two by 0.37). One unit had tubes plugged with the count unrecorded; its tube-side pressure drop, up about 26 percent at a given flow, gives the open fraction, the actual area and the tube velocity (ignoring it misses by 0.16). One unit got a replacement bundle with the baffle spacing widened from 300 to 400 mm and no thermal test; its shell film follows the glycol crossflow velocity to the power 0.6, so its 2025 shell film scaled by 0.75^0.6 settles it (its 2025 test as it stands misses by 0.07).

Three units cannot be settled from the records, and there are three clean-and-test slots. The new unit has only the vendor's optimistic datasheet (0.08 off). One unit's 2025 test sheet does not close its heat balance: the water-side duty is 5 percent below the glycol side, where every other sheet closes within about 1 percent. No single reading repaired from the other side's duty, nor the mean of the two duties, recovers it (0.13 to 0.26 off), and its clean performance is far from its sisters' (the design split or the sisters' median factor miss by 0.09), so only a test settles it. The three units retubed with ribbed tubes have no clean test since. The rib height differs by tube lot, so one unit's measured gain carried to the others misses by 0.08; but ribs raise the tube friction and the tube film together, every unit's pressure drop shows its own friction gain against its 2025 test, and one tested ribbed unit gives the ratio between the two gains. The reactor-jacket unit is ribbed and never stands by, so its RTD offset is unknown too (left untested it misses by 0.27); testing it settles both, and the DCS readings logged with the test show its offset. The bank runs at nearly fixed flows, so an unknown film cannot be fitted from the trend's own flow variation instead (a Wilson plot's own standard error on the test-day value is 0.04 to 0.11, so any agreement is chance). The plan the records seem to point to, testing the new unit, the rebuilt bundle and the reactor-jacket unit, misses the unit with the open sheet; the passing plan tests the new unit, the reactor-jacket unit and the unit with the open sheet, and settles the rebuilt bundle from its baffle spacing.

## Reference solution

solution/solve.py reads the register, the maintenance log, the 2025 acceptance tests, the documents and every trend. Each unit's glycol-outlet offset is the median of outlet minus inlet over its standby hours. Each unit's 2025 clean U (glycol-side duty over the counterflow LMTD, at the design area) fixes one common factor on the design sheet's film split, and the exponents 0.8 on tube velocity and 0.6 on glycol crossflow velocity carry it to any flows; for E5 the shell film is further scaled by (300/400)^0.6 for the replacement bundle's wider baffle spacing, read from the bundle shop note. From the records it lists what they cannot settle: no site clean test (E10), a 2025 sheet whose water-side and glycol-side duties differ by more than 2 percent (E9, 5 percent), and a ribbed unit that never stands by (E8); each gets a clean-and-test slot, and a tested unit's as-found fouling resistance is 1/U as found minus 1/U clean from the reference instruments. For E8 the clean test also gives its tube film (clean 1/U minus the shell film and wall), its film gain over its 2025 tube film, and with its friction gain (tube-side dp at a given flow over its 2025 test) the ratio between the two gains (0.62). For E2 the dp ratio to its 2025 test, to the power -1/1.8, is the open fraction of tubes. For every untested unit, every running hour gives U from the offset-corrected glycol-side duty over the LMTD at its actual area, the clean U at that hour's flows (with the ribbed film gain from the unit's own friction gain where ribbed, and the higher tube velocity where tubes are plugged), and their difference in 1/U; a least-squares line in time with a step at the shock chlorination in the maintenance log (that day's hours left out) carries it to 2026-10-26. On the shipped bank every unit is within 0.019 m2K/kW. It never reads the sealed definition or the verifier.

`solution/solve.sh` starts the service, runs `solve.py` (a few seconds) and checks that the file was written.

## Verification

The verifier holds the bank's sealed definition (tests/hx.py, seed 11), the same module that drives the clean-and-test service. One test gates the reward: /app/output/fouling.csv must have the columns unit and fouling_m2k_per_kw, one row for each of E1-E10 and no others, finite numbers, and every unit's value must be within 0.04 m2K/kW of its true as-found fouling resistance on 2026-10-26. The per-unit record goes to /logs/verifier/metrics.json and never gates. The verifier never runs the reference solution and never reads the shipped files.

The 0.04 m2K/kW is process engineering's requirement, stated in the documents. The evidence in authoring/evidence shows it is wide against the spread of sound methods and narrow against wrong ones (worst unit's error, m2K/kW). method_spread.txt: 108 sound estimates (the passing allocation in every run order; the 2025 clean test split by a common factor or wholly onto either film; median or mean standby offset; a line with the shock step over four or two weeks, or a line through the hours after the shock only) all pass, the worst at 0.020. route_study.txt: offset not removed 0.15, fouling not carried to the test day 0.09 to 0.13, the shock step ignored 0.07 to 0.37, plugging ignored 0.16, ribbed gain not applied 0.18, one ribbed unit's gain carried to the others 0.08, the rebuilt bundle's 2025 test used as it stands 0.07, the vendor datasheet used for the new unit 0.08, the open 2025 sheet used or repaired in any of seven ways 0.13 to 0.26 or set aside for the design split or the sisters' median 0.09, a ribbed unit other than the reactor-jacket unit tested 0.27, an unknown film fitted from the trend (Wilson plot) 0.15 to 0.46 on the new unit and the open-sheet unit, with the fit's own standard error 0.04 to 0.11 (on the reactor-jacket unit the tube fit lands at 0.002 by chance: wilson_noise.txt re-draws only the measurement noise and moves that error between -0.25 and +0.53; the shell fit misses by 0.25). campaign_study.txt: of the 120 ways to spend the three slots, with every fallback above for what each leaves untested, one passes: the new unit, the reactor-jacket unit and the unit with the open sheet; every other allocation misses by at least 0.081. constant_null.txt: the ten true values are irregular and at least 0.091 apart (0.104 to 1.283 m2K/kW), wider than the 0.08 window, so no single constant satisfies more than one unit; zero, 0.35 and the bank's median (0.644) satisfy none. Nor can a unit be filled in from the spacing of the others: with the other nine known exactly, the midpoint of its neighbours (or one median gap beyond the end) misses six units, all three that need a test among them, by 0.05 to 0.10. Every unit therefore has to be assessed on its own, and a submission passes only if all ten are.

The clean-and-test service runs in the agent's container as a separate user, contractor. The agent runs as the user agent ([agent] user); the service's code, the sealed definition and the slot count are owned by contractor with mode 700, so the agent cannot read them, signal the service or edit its state. The agent starts the service with start-hxtest, which runs one fixed script as contractor through a single sudo rule that admits no arguments and passes no environment. Slots are charged before the test is made, so restarting the service does not reset the count; reports are written to /app/test_reports. /app is owned by root with the sticky bit (group-writable for agent), so the agent can create its own files there but cannot rename or replace /app/test_reports or /app/data. The service opens /app/test_reports once at start and refuses it unless it owns it and no one else can write to it; each report is created there as a new file, never through a link and never over an existing file. The slot record is read without following links, the slots left are recomputed from the list of tests, and a record that cannot be read or does not add up counts as no slots left; changes to it are locked across threads and processes. The verifier runs separately with no network, and /logs/verifier is root-only.

Evidence (`authoring/evidence/`): `methods.py` is the shared engine (it runs tests with the service's own function
and uses the sealed definition only for that and for grading); `route_study.py`, `method_spread.py` and
`campaign_study.py`, `constant_null.py` and `wilson_noise.py` write the five `.txt` files; `preflight.py` checks structure, names, ASCII, the sealed
module's three copies, and that `authoring/provenance/gen.py` regenerates the records byte for byte.
