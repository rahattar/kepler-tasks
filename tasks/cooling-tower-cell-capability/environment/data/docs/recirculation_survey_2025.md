# CT-2 inlet air survey, August 2025 (summary)

Aspirated psychrometers were placed at the air inlets of all eight cells for three weeks and compared with the plant met station.

- Cells C1 to C7: inlet wet bulb within 0.2 K of the met station's in every wind direction.
- Cell C8, at the east end of the row: when the wind blew from the west (from 225 to 315 degrees), part of the warm, saturated plume from the row was drawn back into its inlet, and its inlet wet bulb ran 2.0 K above the met station's on average. In other wind directions it matched the met station.

No change has been made to the tower since the survey that would affect this.
