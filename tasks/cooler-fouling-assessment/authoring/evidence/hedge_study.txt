conclusion: each reading is settled in the documents, not left to the solver's judgement. The retube
note ties the film gain to the friction gain through one coefficient of the rib form, so the single
tested ribbed unit fixes that coefficient and every other ribbed unit follows from its own dp; the
design sheet ties the shell-side crossflow velocity to the baffle spacing, so the rebuilt bundle's
shell film follows from its new spacing. Read that way there is one figure per unit, and it is the one
the records support. The gaps between readings are under 2 x the requirement, so a number split
between two readings could in principle satisfy both: that is why the requirement appears in no file
the solver can read, and a split answer cannot be aimed at a band it cannot see.
