# E5 replacement bundle (bundle shop job BS-2026-117)

The original E5 bundle was retired in August 2026 with corroded baffles. The replacement was built to the revised
baffle drawing C-200-B rev 3: same shell, tube count, tube size, tube layout and pitch, and the same 25 percent baffle
cut, with thicker baffles and the baffle spacing increased from 300 mm to 400 mm to lower the shell-side pressure drop.
Hydrotested at the shop; no thermal performance test has been made on the new bundle.
