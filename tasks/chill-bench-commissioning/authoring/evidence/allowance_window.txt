The tolerance scales almost linearly with the allowance. At 20% any covariate
model that gets the shape roughly right lands inside it; at 8% the station
coefficient has to be known to within about six percent, which is the scale on
which the prediction errors of competent eight-run campaigns actually lie
(design_mc.txt, baselines.txt). The highest-coefficient stations are the least
tolerant, because d ln f / d ln a = 2/(2 - a) grows with the coefficient. At 6%
the reference's 1.5% margin against its own optimum estimate is already a
quarter of the allowance.
