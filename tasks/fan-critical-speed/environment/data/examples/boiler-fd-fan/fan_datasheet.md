# Boiler FD Fan - OEM datasheet (as built)

- Single inlet centrifugal fan, overhung impeller, direct drive from a 4-pole motor through a flexible disc coupling; variable speed drive, maximum continuous speed 1490 rpm.
- Shaft drawing FS-4111 rev A; sections from the coupling end of the shaft:

| from (mm) | to (mm) | diameter (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 300 | 100 |
| 300 | 500 | 130 |
| 500 | 1500 | 150 |
| 1500 | 1700 | 130 |
| 1700 | 2150 | 120 |

- Bearings: spherical roller bearings, drive end at 400 mm and fan end at 1600 mm (bearing centerlines); radial stiffness 0.90 GN/m each.
- Pedestals: two cast pedestals of one design, effective mass 200 kg each at the bearing centerline.
- Coupling hub (fan half): center of gravity at 60 mm; mass 24.0 kg; transverse moment of inertia 0.35 kg m2; polar moment of inertia 0.60 kg m2.
- Impeller: center of gravity at the shaft end, 2150 mm; mass 420 kg; transverse moment of inertia about the center of gravity 44.7 kg m2; polar moment of inertia (WR2) 81.3 kg m2.
