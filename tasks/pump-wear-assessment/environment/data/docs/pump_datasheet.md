# CW-250 cooling-water pump: datasheet extract

Single-stage, double-suction volute pump; impeller 330 mm as supplied; 1480 rpm induction motor.

| item | value |
|---|---|
| rated flow | 900 m3/h |
| rated head | 45 m |
| shutoff head | about 58 m |
| best efficiency | about 84 % near rated flow |

- Head in this document, on the factory certificates and on the shop rig is the pump total head between the
  suction and discharge flanges: static pressure rise plus the velocity-head difference, in metres of water
  (998 kg/m3).
- Speed changes follow the affinity laws (flow with speed, head with speed squared). Small impeller trims follow
  the trim law: flow scales with the diameter ratio, head with its square.
- Wear-ring wear lets water recirculate from discharge to suction. On this model the head it costs is, to within
  the accuracy of a site test, the same fraction of the as-new head at every flow in the operating range; the
  rings on the bank were last renewed between 2019 and 2021 and, in normal service, wear over years, not weeks.
