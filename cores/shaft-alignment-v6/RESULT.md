v5 is v2 with one defect removed: the maker's gearbox lateral figure had no stated direction, which gave a
careful run a real reason to spend a visit on a compressor. v6 is v5 with the laser programme placed in a
single order before any report exists.

**v3 and v4: too well hidden.** With the split inside one model no run at either effort covered it in time.
Nine of the fifteen spent the spare on a compressor and two on the pump whose grout was repaired. Several saw
the 0.38 and 0.47 once they were carrying a measured figure across, with nothing left to spend.

**v2 and v5: the low-effort passes came from ordering one visit at a time.** All three low-effort passes
bought one pump of each listed class first, read the reports, and only then looked at how alike the blanked
pumps had been. The single order takes that away: the sixth train has to be chosen on the records.

**v6, low effort, six of six the same.** Every run named TP-301A, TP-301B, one lagged pump, one blanked
foot-mounted pump, one blanked centreline pump, and K-201A. The stated reason each time: three compressors
with complaints since April and a gearbox figure from 2017. Each then carried one blanked figure across both
HSC models and missed P-320A/B or P-322 and P-325A/B by 0.36 to 0.44 mm against 0.05. Three of the six named
the 0.38 and 0.47 in their report as the weak point, after the order was placed.

**v6, xhigh.** One pass in ten minutes, on the intended argument made before ordering: "the two models cooled
differently (0.38 against 0.47), so each needed its own visit", and of the centreline pair, "both models had
the same cooled share (0.16)", and of the compressors, "AP-12 says to use the maker's figure, and the
gearboxes weren't in the revamp". Two failures, both by naming K-201A.

**What this is, in the trap analysis's terms.** Kind A, with the difference that the class the run fails to
cover is not one it wasted a visit instead of, but one it never counted: the records give five needs for six
places, and the sixth is a ratio in a seven-year-old survey. It has all four shared properties: no cell names
the split as a need; the wrong route is confirmed to a micrometre by the visit it wastes; the miss is 7 to 9
times the tolerance on the same pumps in every run; nothing is fitted, so no residual shows it.

**Not established.**
- 0 of 6 bounds the low-effort solve rate below about 0.39 at 95 per cent. 0 of 12 would bound it below 0.22.
- 1 of 3 at xhigh is one run. The difficulty screen wants 1 to 7 of 8.
- None of this has met the official screen.
- Probe 1 of v6 reported listing `/var/tmp` by mistake. The bare sandbox keeps the rig there in the open; the
  container will not.
