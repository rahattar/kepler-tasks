# Kiln ID Fan - OEM datasheet (as built)

- Single inlet centrifugal fan, overhung impeller, direct drive from a 6-pole motor through a flexible disc coupling; variable speed drive, maximum continuous speed 990 rpm.
- Shaft drawing FS-4121 rev A; sections from the coupling end of the shaft:

| from (mm) | to (mm) | diameter (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 300 | 100 |
| 300 | 500 | 125 |
| 500 | 1700 | 145 |
| 1700 | 1900 | 125 |
| 1900 | 2400 | 115 |

- Bearings: spherical roller bearings, drive end at 400 mm and fan end at 1800 mm (bearing centerlines); radial stiffness 1.10 GN/m each.
- Pedestals: two cast pedestals of one design, effective mass 280 kg each at the bearing centerline.
- Coupling hub (fan half): center of gravity at 60 mm; mass 24.0 kg; transverse moment of inertia 0.35 kg m2; polar moment of inertia 0.60 kg m2.
- Impeller: center of gravity at the shaft end, 2400 mm; mass 480 kg; transverse moment of inertia about the center of gravity 55.9 kg m2; polar moment of inertia (WR2) 101.6 kg m2.
