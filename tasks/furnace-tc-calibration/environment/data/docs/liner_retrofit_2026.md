# SiC liner retrofit (2026): engineering change summary

Silicon carbide liners replaced the quartz liners in F2, F3 and F7 (April to June 2026) to reduce particle counts.
The liner sits between the heater and the boat, so it changes how the wafers exchange radiation with the heater:
the wafer-to-profile offset changes, by different amounts in different zones. The liners are slip-cast; their wall
thickness varies along a liner and from one liner to the next (incoming inspection: 3.1 to 5.6 mm). The 2025
TC-wafer corrections remain loaded on these furnaces; recalibration is part of this year's bay calibration. The
heaters, the profile assemblies and their positions were not changed.
