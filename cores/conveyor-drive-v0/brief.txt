Work only inside PROBE. Do not read, list or open anything outside PROBE. Everything you need is under PROBE/app.

A mining company runs its ore on lines of belt conveyors and calculates their drives to the company's standard BC-3, `PROBE/app/data/docs/bc3_conveyor_drives.md`: from the feed log of a line, the mean, greatest and least motor power and the peak traction ratio of every drive in every interval of the log. A line sends its records in as a package, in the format of `PROBE/app/data/docs/line_package.md`.

Write `PROBE/app/output/drives.py`, the program that makes this calculation for one package. It is run as `python3 drives.py LINE_DIR OUT_CSV` and writes `OUT_CSV` with the header `row,mean_kW,max_kW,min_kW,traction_peak` and one row for every drive and interval of the package, named as `line_package.md` names the rows of `expected.csv`, the values as decimal numbers.

Two packages with their results to BC-3 are in `PROBE/app/data/examples/quarry_line` and `PROBE/app/data/examples/ridge_haul`, and a package without results is in `PROBE/app/data/pending/pit_incline`.

The program is then run on the packages of six other lines, in the same format, of up to four flights and 17,280 log steps. Each run of drives.py must finish within 30 seconds on 1 CPU. For every row of every package, the mean, the greatest and the least power must each be within 4 per cent of the drive's rated power of the result to BC-3, and the peak traction within 3 per cent of it.

Each run takes place in a scratch directory holding a copy of `drives.py` alone, so the program cannot read any other file you leave, and must end with exit status 0. Python 3 with numpy, scipy and pandas is available.

This machine is shared with other jobs, so judge your program's speed by its CPU time, not by the wall clock. Write any scratch files under PROBE/app/work. When you are done, report what you did, how you checked it and how long a run takes.
