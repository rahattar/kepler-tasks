# Kestrel Lane rising main - system description

Pump station PS-2 lifts treated water from its wet well to the service reservoir through a single rising main.

## Pump station PS-2
- Wet well water level 14.0 m AOD (held by the inlet control).
- Duty pumps discharge through individual non-return valves into a common header. Header centreline 12.0 m AOD.
- Pressure transmitter PT201 on the header, 12.6 m AOD. Electromagnetic flowmeter FT201 on the rising main.
- The existing pumps are to be replaced by two Halvorsen sets (pump_datasheets.md), two sets running together at all times, and a surge vessel is to be added on the header.

## Rising main
- Ductile iron, cement-mortar lined, internal diameter 600 mm, PN16; 5,200 m from PS-2 to the reservoir inlet. Wave speed 1,100 m/s (manufacturer's value for the pipe class and lining).
- Design friction: Colebrook-White with roughness 0.15 mm.

## Service reservoir
- Inlet at chainage 5,200 m, discharging below the water surface. Operating levels: bottom water level 75.0 m AOD, top water level 78.0 m AOD. Level transmitter LT501 (m AOD).
